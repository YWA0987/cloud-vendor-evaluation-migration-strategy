# Exit costs and vendor lock-in

This page summarizes the original report's separate analysis of a possible **post-year-three exit from Microsoft Azure**. It is **not included** in the primary three-year modeled TCO of $243,741.

| Exit-related allowance | Scenario amount |
|:--|--:|
| Transition work, retraining and reconfiguration | $31,600 |
| Unexpected-expense allowance | $6,320 |
| Data-transfer charges | $1,500 |
| **Total separate exit allowance** | **$39,420** |

These amounts are **planning assumptions**, not supplier quotations. The submitted report notes that real exit costs may also depend on application changes, contractual and licensing terms, business disruption, replacement-provider charges, and data-export procedures.

## Management recommendations

1. Record infrastructure and application configurations so critical components can be recreated.
2. Verify whether exported data can be restored outside the selected platform.
3. Review contract cancellation clauses, licensing restrictions, and egress costs before committing.
4. Track platform-specific dependencies and the costs of replacing them.
5. Treat the potential exit as a **separate decision scenario**, not as a hidden recurring cost.

**Provenance:** Based on the submitted October 25, 2026 MGS402 memo, section "Exit Costs and Vendor Lock-In". All figures apply only to the fictional Lakefront Retail scenario.
